# Privacy policy — Museum Wallpapers for macOS

Museum Wallpapers does not collect, store or send any personal information.
There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no telemetry.

To find and show paintings, the app connects only to Wikimedia services:
`commons.wikimedia.org`, `upload.wikimedia.org`, `www.wikidata.org`,
`query.wikidata.org` and `*.wikipedia.org`. Wikimedia's own privacy
policy applies to them: https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy

To look for updates, the app asks GitHub (`api.github.com`) once a day for
the latest release of this repository, and downloads an update from
`github.com` only when you click Install. You can turn the daily check off
in the menu. GitHub's privacy statement applies:
https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

All requests carry a User-Agent naming the app and nothing about you.

Everything the app keeps stays on your Mac: its settings
(`~/Library/Application Support/MuseumWallpapers`), the downloaded paintings
(`~/Library/Caches/MuseumWallpapers`), a log file
(`~/Library/Logs/MuseumWallpapers`), and the paintings you save in
`~/Pictures/Museum Wallpapers`.

Questions: https://github.com/dpsk/museum-wallpapers-macos/issues
